Database Entities

Our normalized database consists of eight main entities, each representing a distinct concept in the food delivery domain. Each entity has specific attributes and participates in relationships with other entities.

Customer

CustomerID (PK)
CustomerName

Represents customers who place food orders through the platform.

CustomerPhone

CustomerID (FK, PK)
PhoneNumber (PK)

Stores multiple phone numbers for each customer (weak entity).

Restaurant

RestaurantID (PK)
RestaurantName
Location
ContactNumber

Contains information about restaurants participating in the delivery service.

Rider

RiderID (PK)
RiderName
ContactNumber
VehicleType

Represents delivery riders who transport orders from restaurants to customers.

Orders

OrderID (PK)
CustomerID (FK)
RestaurantID (FK)
RiderID (FK)
OrderDate
TotalAmount

Main entity representing food delivery orders (central entity).

Food

FoodID (PK)
FoodName
Description
Price

Contains information about individual food items available for order.

OrderFood

OrderID (FK, PK)
FoodID (FK, PK)
Quantity

Junction table for many-to-many relationship between Orders and Food.

Payment

PaymentID (PK)
OrderID (FK)
PaymentMethod
Amount
PaymentDate

Records payment transactions for completed orders.
